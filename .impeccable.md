## Design Context

### Users
Minimalists who like to think. They use the inbox to consume important ideas with low visual noise and high information density.

### Brand Personality
Simple, insightful, productive.

### Aesthetic Direction
Restrained and editorial. Quiet surfaces, clear text hierarchy, strong alignment, and minimal decoration. Prioritize legibility and focus over personality flourishes.

### Design Principles
1. Let content carry the weight; remove any visual treatment that competes with reading.
2. Use typography, spacing, and contrast to create hierarchy instead of extra chrome.
3. Favor source clarity and reading flow over metadata density.
4. Keep interactions calm and predictable, with subtle state changes only.
5. Preserve compactness, but never at the expense of readability.
